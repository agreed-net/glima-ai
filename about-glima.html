Our Story

Glima started with a simple vision: make powerful AI creativity accessible to everyone. We bring image, video, audio, and editing tools together in one intuitive platform, helping creators, teams, and businesses turn ideas into exceptional content faster and easier.

From images and videos to audio, creative effects, and AI-powered editing, Glima brings modern AI models and practical creative tools together in a workflow designed for everyone — from first-time creators to professional teams.
